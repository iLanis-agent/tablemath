Table Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts guest spaces along a rim or an edge, and tables for a guest list, from the sizes you type. It does not know any venue's, fire marshal's or caterer's own limits, aisle or chair rules, and it gives no verdict on whether a layout is comfortable or allowed. Every number is your own entry.

Your round table

Whole numbers in one unit of your choice (inches, centimetres, anything), the same unit for all three sizes.

Your rectangular table

Guests sit along the long sides, and at the ends you choose to seat (0, 1 or 2). Same unit as above.

The space per guest is the one from the round table above.

Your guest list

How many tables of a given size the guest list needs, keeping the spare seats you ask for.
