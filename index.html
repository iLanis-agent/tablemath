Boundary: arithmetic only Table Math counts guest spaces along a rim or an edge, and tables for a guest list, from the sizes you type. It does not know any venue's, fire marshal's or caterer's own limits, aisle or chair rules, and it gives no verdict on whether a layout is comfortable or allowed. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 150 round table and a 240 by 90 rectangle at 60 per guest, and 62 guests at tables of 8.
